What we forecast预测对象

Each series represents one checkpoint and one visitor group.每条序列对应一个口岸和一种访客类别。

The target is the daily number of inbound visitors at checkpoint p for visitor group g on Hong Kong calendar day d. We use the mainland visitor and other visitor columns from public arrival records. The rehearsal uses 10 proxy checkpoints with complete histories in the 30 November 2025 archive. The organizers will specify the official checkpoints.因变量是口岸 p、客群 g、香港日历日 d 的入境访客人次。公开原始数据取入境记录中的内地访客和其他访客两列。赛前模拟用 2025-11-30 归档选定 10 个历史完整的代理口岸。正式口岸以组委会数据为准。

Archived rehearsal models归档模拟的两种模型

Both models generate a point forecast and a 70% interval for each checkpoint, group and day.两种模型都输出逐口岸、逐客群、逐日的点预测及 70% 区间。

Four-week same-weekday mean四周同星期均值

To predict next Saturday, average arrivals from the four most recent Saturdays before the cutoff for the same checkpoint and visitor group.预测下周六时，取数据截止前最近四个星期六的同口岸、同客群实绩求均值。

Point forecast = mean arrivals on the last four matching weekdays点预测 = 最近四个同一星期几的平均人次

Repeat the forecast at weekly cutoffs in the preceding six months. Add the 15th and 85th percentiles of observed minus predicted arrivals to the point forecast to form the interval. Floor its lower bound at zero.在截止前约半年内，每隔七天重做一组历史预测，以实绩减预测得到误差。将误差的第 15 和第 85 百分位数分别加到点预测上，得到区间上下限。低于零的下限设为零。

Chronos-2 + calendarChronos-2 加日历

A pretrained time-series transformer reads all 20 arrivals series and the calendar together and predicts future quantiles for each series. The model can use historical patterns shared across checkpoints.用预训练时间序列 Transformer 同时读取 20 条客流序列及日历，直接生成每条序列未来各日的分位数预测。模型可以利用同一口岸和不同口岸之间的历史共变。

Point forecast = 50th percentile
70% interval = 15th to 85th percentile点预测取第 50 百分位数。70% 区间取第 15 至第 85 百分位数。

The rehearsals use model weights publicly available at the historical cutoff. Forecast counts are bounded below by zero.历史模拟使用当时已公开的模型权重做推理。输出人数取非负值。

Rehearsal and scoring怎么验证预测

We move the competition dates back one year. Each round has three same-day data archives and one 15-day submission window.正式赛程向前平移一年。五轮各有三份当日归档和同一个 15 天预测窗口。

  1. Retrieve the archive取得归档Use counts available before noon on the simulated date, with observations through the prior day.按模拟日 12:00 前可见的数据取客流，历史止于前一日。
  2. Generate forecasts生成预测Update at each release. Forecast two extra days in the first release and one in the second, then align to the submission window.根据每次发布更新。早期两版各多预测 2 天和 1 天，再对齐同一提交窗口。
  3. Freeze the third forecast锁定第三版Use the third release for each rehearsal submission. Read the outcomes after freezing the forecasts.每轮用第三版模拟提交。冻结全部预测后，再读取实绩。
  4. Score the forecasts独立评分Score 280 targets on days 2–15. Rank and select the best four rounds separately for point and interval scores.第 2–15 天共 280 个目标。点和区间分别选各自得分最好的四轮。
Point forecasts · MASE点预测 · MASE
Divide each absolute error by the mean absolute seven-day difference in the corresponding series history, then average. Lower is better.将每个绝对预测误差除以对应序列的历史七日差分绝对值均值，再取平均。数值越低越好。
Intervals · Mean scaled Winkler区间预测 · 平均缩放 Winkler
Combine interval width with a penalty for observations outside the interval and divide by the same scale. Lower is better.同时衡量区间宽度和实际值落在区间外时的罚分，再用同一尺度缩放。数值越低越好。
Interval coverage区间覆盖率
The share of observed counts inside the 70% forecast interval. Chronos-2 + calendar covered 66.4% across the five rehearsals.实绩落在 70% 预测区间内的比例，用来检查区间是否合适。Chronos-2 加日历本次五轮覆盖率为 66.4%。

The rehearsals use proxy checkpoints with complete historical records. Official forecasts will follow the published checkpoint list, templates and final scoring guide.归档模拟使用历史完整的代理口岸。正式预测采用组委会发布的口岸、模板及最终评分指南。

Two representations, three fusion positions两类表征，三个融合位置

Using the same daily and weekly encoder, compare direct media embeddings and aligned cues at the input, intermediate representation and forecast stages. The retrospective reconstructs cues by posting time.在同一个日与周时序骨干上，比较直接输入图文视频向量和输入对齐线索。分别在输入层、中间表示或预测后融合。历史对照按发帖时间重建线索。

Cue-masked attention uses the frozen arrivals state to query eligible media roles and learn a bounded residual. In a separate attention experiment with daily training origins, five-round mean MASE was 0.962, compared with 0.959 for arrivals and calendar and 0.971 for early fusion of aligned cues.线索掩码注意力以冻结的客流状态为查询，选择有效模态并学习有界修正。在每日训练起点的独立注意力实验中，五轮平均 MASE 为 0.962，同批客流加日历为 0.959，线索早期融合为 0.971。

Proposed parallel evidence verification拟议的并行证据核验

Separate events, travel intent and checkpoint access into evidence streams, retaining provenance and disagreements.把活动、出行意图和口岸通达性分给不同证据分支，保留各自出处和分歧。

Agents would produce structured records with sources, availability times and effective dates. The custom graph model would produce the forecasts. Matched-budget comparisons would test single, sequential and independent parallel agents on both cue accuracy and forecast errors.代理输出有来源、可见时间和适用日期的结构化记录，自研图模型负责数值预测。拟对比同预算的单代理、串行代理和独立并行代理，分别检验线索准确性和最终预测误差。
