归档模拟的两种模型Archived rehearsal models

两种模型都输出逐口岸、逐客群、逐日的点预测及 70% 区间。Both models generate a point forecast and a 70% interval for each checkpoint, group and day.

四周同星期均值Four-week same-weekday mean

预测下周六时，取数据截止前最近四个星期六的同口岸、同客群实绩求均值。To predict next Saturday, average arrivals from the four most recent Saturdays before the cutoff for the same checkpoint and visitor group.

点预测 = 最近四个同一星期几的平均人次Point forecast = mean arrivals on the last four matching weekdays

在截止前约半年内，每隔七天重做一组历史预测，以实绩减预测得到误差。将误差的第 15 和第 85 百分位数分别加到点预测上，得到区间上下限。低于零的下限设为零。Repeat the forecast at weekly cutoffs in the preceding six months. Add the 15th and 85th percentiles of observed minus predicted arrivals to the point forecast to form the interval. Floor its lower bound at zero.

Chronos-2 加日历Chronos-2 + calendar

用预训练时间序列 Transformer 同时读取 20 条客流序列及日历，直接生成每条序列未来各日的分位数预测。模型可以利用同一口岸和不同口岸之间的历史共变。A pretrained time-series transformer reads all 20 arrivals series and the calendar together and predicts future quantiles for each series. The model can use historical patterns shared across checkpoints.

点预测 = 第 50 百分位数
70% 区间 = 第 15 至第 85 百分位数Point forecast = 50th percentile
70% interval = 15th to 85th percentile

历史模拟使用当时已公开的模型权重做推理。输出人数取非负值。The rehearsals use model weights publicly available at the historical cutoff. Forecast counts are bounded below by zero.

怎么验证预测Rehearsal and scoring

正式赛程向前平移一年。五轮各有三份当日归档和同一个 15 天预测窗口。We move the competition dates back one year. Each round has three same-day data archives and one 15-day submission window.

  1. 取得归档Retrieve the archive按模拟日 12:00 前可见的数据取客流，历史止于前一日。Use counts available before noon on the simulated date, with observations through the prior day.
  2. 生成预测Generate forecasts根据每次发布更新。早期两版各多预测 2 天和 1 天，再对齐同一提交窗口。Update at each release. Forecast two extra days in the first release and one in the second, then align to the submission window.
  3. 锁定第三版Freeze the third forecast每轮用第三版模拟提交。冻结全部预测后，再读取实绩。Use the third release for each rehearsal submission. Read the outcomes after freezing the forecasts.
  4. 独立评分Score the forecasts第 2–15 天共 280 个目标。点和区间分别选各自得分最好的四轮。Score 280 targets on days 2–15. Rank and select the best four rounds separately for point and interval scores.
点预测 · MASEPoint forecasts · MASE
将每个绝对预测误差除以对应序列的历史七日差分绝对值均值，再取平均。数值越低越好。Divide each absolute error by the mean absolute seven-day difference in the corresponding series history, then average. Lower is better.
区间预测 · 平均缩放 WinklerIntervals · Mean scaled Winkler
同时衡量区间宽度和实际值落在区间外时的罚分，再用同一尺度缩放。数值越低越好。Combine interval width with a penalty for observations outside the interval and divide by the same scale. Lower is better.
区间覆盖率Interval coverage
实绩落在 70% 预测区间内的比例，用来检查区间是否合适。Chronos-2 加日历本次五轮覆盖率为 66.4%。The share of observed counts inside the 70% forecast interval. Chronos-2 + calendar covered 66.4% across the five rehearsals.

归档模拟使用历史完整的代理口岸。正式预测采用组委会发布的口岸、模板及最终评分指南。The rehearsals use proxy checkpoints with complete historical records. Official forecasts will follow the published checkpoint list, templates and final scoring guide.

图文视频怎样进模型How text, images and video enter the model

识别确认预订、赴港计划、定期活动、入境路线、服务变更与排队。按明确日期、客群、口岸对齐，逐条记录首次取得时间。配图、封面和视频帧用 CLIP 表示场景。Identify confirmed bookings, inbound plans, scheduled events, crossing routes, service changes and queues. Align explicit dates, visitor groups and checkpoints. Record first retrieval and use CLIP to represent scenes in photos, covers and video frames.

两类表征，三个融合位置Two representations, three fusion positions

在同一个日与周时序骨干上，比较直接输入图文视频向量和输入对齐线索。分别在输入层、中间表示或预测后融合。历史对照按发帖时间重建线索。Using the same daily and weekly encoder, compare direct media embeddings and aligned cues at the input, intermediate representation and forecast stages. The retrospective reconstructs cues by posting time.

线索掩码注意力以冻结的客流状态为查询，选择有效模态并学习有界修正。在每日训练起点的独立注意力实验中，五轮平均 MASE 为 0.962，同批客流加日历为 0.959，线索早期融合为 0.971。Cue-masked attention uses the frozen arrivals state to query eligible media roles and learn a bounded residual. In a separate attention experiment with daily training origins, five-round mean MASE was 0.962, compared with 0.959 for arrivals and calendar and 0.971 for early fusion of aligned cues.

拟议的并行证据核验Proposed parallel evidence verification

把活动、出行意图和口岸通达性分给不同证据分支，保留各自出处和分歧。Separate events, travel intent and checkpoint access into evidence streams, retaining provenance and disagreements.

代理输出有来源、可见时间和适用日期的结构化记录，自研图模型负责数值预测。拟对比同预算的单代理、串行代理和独立并行代理，分别检验线索准确性和最终预测误差。Agents would produce structured records with sources, availability times and effective dates. The custom graph model would produce the forecasts. Matched-budget comparisons would test single, sequential and independent parallel agents on both cue accuracy and forecast errors.
