What we forecast

Each series represents one checkpoint and one visitor group.

The target is the daily number of inbound visitors at checkpoint p for visitor group g on Hong Kong calendar day d. We use the mainland visitor and other visitor columns from public arrival records. The rehearsal uses 10 proxy checkpoints with complete histories in the 30 November 2025 archive. The organizers will specify the official checkpoints.

Archived rehearsal models

Both models generate a point forecast and a 70% interval for each checkpoint, group and day.

Four-week same-weekday mean

To predict next Saturday, average arrivals from the four most recent Saturdays before the cutoff for the same checkpoint and visitor group.

Point forecast = mean arrivals on the last four matching weekdays

Repeat the forecast at weekly cutoffs in the preceding six months. Add the 15th and 85th percentiles of observed minus predicted arrivals to the point forecast to form the interval. Floor its lower bound at zero.

Chronos-2 + calendar

A pretrained time-series transformer reads all 20 arrivals series and the calendar together and predicts future quantiles for each series. The model can use historical patterns shared across checkpoints.

Point forecast = 50th percentile
70% interval = 15th to 85th percentile

The rehearsals use model weights publicly available at the historical cutoff. Forecast counts are bounded below by zero.

Rehearsal and scoring

We move the competition dates back one year. Each round has three same-day data archives and one 15-day submission window.

  1. Retrieve the archiveUse counts available before noon on the simulated date, with observations through the prior day.
  2. Generate forecastsUpdate at each release. Forecast two extra days in the first release and one in the second, then align to the submission window.
  3. Freeze the third forecastUse the third release for each rehearsal submission. Read the outcomes after freezing the forecasts.
  4. Score the forecastsScore 280 targets on days 2–15. Rank and select the best four rounds separately for point and interval scores.
Point forecasts · MASE
Divide each absolute error by the mean absolute seven-day difference in the corresponding series history, then average. Lower is better.
Intervals · Mean scaled Winkler
Combine interval width with a penalty for observations outside the interval and divide by the same scale. Lower is better.
Interval coverage
The share of observed counts inside the 70% forecast interval. Chronos-2 + calendar covered 66.4% across the five rehearsals.

The rehearsals use proxy checkpoints with complete historical records. Official forecasts will follow the published checkpoint list, templates and final scoring guide.

How posts enter the model

We sampled 6,000 posts from X, Weibo, Instagram, Douyin and TikTok. Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B encodes post text. CLIP ViT-B/32 encodes attached photos, thumbnails and sampled frames from original videos. We average frame embeddings within each video.

We pool posts in four seven-day windows before each cutoff. Posts that match a single checkpoint provide local inputs. The remaining posts provide a citywide signal. Cross-attention lets the arrivals and calendar features select information across modalities and windows.

Checkpoint links and 15-day forecasts

Each checkpoint and visitor group pair is a node. The model reads 56 days of arrivals and uses distance as a spatial prior while learning input-dependent links among checkpoints for each group. It adjusts the four-week same-weekday mean and predicts the 15th, 50th and 85th percentiles.

We keep the graph, time windows and scoring targets fixed and train three variants with arrivals and calendars, with added text, and with added text, images and video. We use a validation period near each cutoff for early stopping and hold out the target dates for scoring. Each score averages all five rounds using days 2–15.

Publication dates and collection dates

The retrospective experiment aligns content retrieved later by its posting date. That date cannot verify what each text, image or video looked like at the historical cutoff. The comparison estimates potential incremental value. For the 11–25 November trial, we used posts and checkpoint references available by the forecast cutoff and froze forecasts for all 20 series for later evaluation.
